Beliefs我的一些认识

  1. Small and complete beats big and vague小而完整，好过大而全

    Finishing one thing matters more than sprawling.把一件事做完，比铺一个大摊子重要。

  2. Done only when it's truly used被真正用到，才算做完

    If one person opens it every day, that's why it exists.有一个人每天打开你做的东西，就是它存在的意义。

  3. Polishing repeatedly is a form of care反复打磨，是一种认真

    Wanting to revise yet again — a bad habit, and a standard.改完一版还想再改——坏习惯，也是标准。
